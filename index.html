Our method

The 10-Point Project Management Checklist.

Projects are too often derailed by inadequate coordination. Our checklist curtails errors in the plans and reduces change orders in the field — so your project arrives on budget and on record.

Talk through your project →

  • Zoning Verification

    Confirmed at least twice in preliminary design — by phone and in person at the municipality.

  • Utilities Coordination

    The MEP engineer locates water and sewer connections before we commit the plan.

  • ADA & Code Compliance

    Commercial work is checked against ADA, UFAS, or Fair Housing as the code requires.

  • Construction Type Documentation

    Drawings state construction type, stories, and occupancy per the Florida Building Code.

  • Plus six further checkpoints spanning survey, structural, MEP, life-safety, permitting, and closeout — reviewed on every project.

How we work

A clear path from first sketch to final walkthrough.

Four phases, with one principal alongside you the whole way — so decisions stay informed and nothing slips between the drawings and the field.

  1. 01

    Consultation

    We listen to how you'll use the space, walk the site, and confirm zoning and budget before a line is drawn.

  2. 02

    Design

    Concepts become plans and elevations — resolved for light, flow, and the way you live, reviewed with you at each step.

  3. 03

    Documentation & Permitting

    Construction documents are coordinated for code, ADA, and structure, then carried through municipal review.

  4. 04

    Construction

    We stay involved on site — answering questions and protecting the design intent through to the final walkthrough.

The firm

Three decades of hands-on architecture.

United Architects is led by owner and president Maria Luisa Castellanos, R.A., LEED AP — with more than thirty years of industry experience. Before founding the firm in 1986, she led projects across Miami and internationally in Saudi Arabia, Fiji, and Costa Rica.

A member of the Women's Chamber of Commerce of Miami-Dade County and an adjunct professor of architecture at Miami Dade College, she stays involved — hands-on — through every phase of design and construction.
